Anchoring Bias

The first number you encounter becomes a mental anchor, warping perception of all subsequent values. A house listed at $500K makes $450K feel like a bargain—even if it's worth $350K. Drag the anchor to a new position and watch how the surrounding numbers re-normalize around it. What seemed extreme becomes acceptable once the reference point shifts.
