Narrative Fallacy

We create coherent stories to explain random events. Our minds cannot accept randomness—they weave cause-and-effect tales from noise. Watch events unfold. Notice how you instinctively connect them into a story.

Click and drag between events to draw the "story" you see. Then reveal the truth.

As you watch, your mind will automatically construct a narrative explaining why each event led to the next.
