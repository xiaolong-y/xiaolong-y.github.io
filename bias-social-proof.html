Social Proof

We look to others' behavior to determine correct action. When uncertain, we assume the crowd knows something we don't. Click a door to enter—but watch how the crowd influences which door feels "right."

Two identical restaurants. One is empty, one has a line. Which do you choose? Toggle the crowd to feel the pull.
