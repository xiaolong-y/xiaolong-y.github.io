The Hindsight Illusion
Once you know the outcome, your brain rewrites history. The path that was taken now seems inevitable—the warning signs "obvious," the decision "clearly correct." But those other faded paths? They were just as vivid before you knew the ending.
"Hindsight is 20/20, but foresight was always wearing a blindfold."
